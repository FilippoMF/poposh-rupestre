Wishing tree

This piece represents the process of ecological regeneration, through which degraded land gradually becomes fertile. The sculpture incorporates coin trees, a folk practice in which coins are hammered into the bark of a tree as a way of connecting with it.

Charred sycamore wood, coins, wood stain, wood shavings

2025

Dimensions: 20 × 20 × 110 cm
