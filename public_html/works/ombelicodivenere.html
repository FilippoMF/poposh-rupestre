l'Ombelico di Venere

This sculpture is a reinterpretation of archaic fertility figurines found across the Mediterranean. Often depicted as female forms, they represent fertility in a broader sense, encompassing all living beings, and embody an animistic worldview.

Ash wood, seashell, ceramic, indian ink, wood stain

2025

Dimensions: 4 × 26 × 100 cm
