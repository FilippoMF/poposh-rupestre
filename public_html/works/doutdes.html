Do Ut Des

This piece is about reciprocity. The reciprocity that you see all around us in the natural world: the dead matter that you give, and the new life that you get in return. This sculpture was placed inside an ancient Ligurian Apuan tomb called “Cavaneo”.

Shark vertebras, charred oak wood, wild clay fired with wood

2024

Dimensions: 90 × 27 × 27 cm
