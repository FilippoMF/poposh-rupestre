Corrente Madre

"Corrente Madre" is a graphic novel about sailing trip across the Tyrrhenian Sea. As they navigate its waters, they are transported back to their childhoods and begin to confront the challenges of transitioning into adulthood. At its core, the story reflects on our place in the world, urging a more humble understanding of our role as humans in relation to other living beings and the natural forces that shape life. Ultimately, it’s a tale about growing up, learning, and embracing the unpredictable flow of life.

China ink on cotton paper
