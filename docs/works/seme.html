Seme

This artwork represents the transmission of life. Like many plants, this sculpture is modular, with repeated forms that replicate and grow into an increasingly complex structure, symbolising the trans- mission and development of the living world. At the base, there is a nativity scene of all life forms. At the top, a seed rests in the womb of the next generation.

Oak wood, lichens, dyes, wild clay fired with wood

2024

Dimensions: 110 × 30 × 20 cm
