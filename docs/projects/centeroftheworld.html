A Journey to the Center of the World

The artworks that are part of this project were made during a residency I organised at APUA Space, which was dedicated to the subterranean worlds and stories that lie beneath our feet.

This project was undertaken in partnership with the geological foundation Fontazione Promonte Caprione and hosted by Azienda Agricola Pian della Chiesa. These artworks were part of a larger body of work that was made collectively by the group of artists that participated adn exhibited at during a final restituition event.
