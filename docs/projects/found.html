Found imaginaries

Found imaginaries is a series of sculptural works that explore the imagineries that can emerge from the unknown. The three works in this series are created starting from curious-looking objects that I have collected over the years: a mangrove seed, iron ore pebbles and a split limestone. It is when we are confronted with the mysterious and the unknown that our imagination, stories and myths are born. Here, these three artworks explore stories of growth and transformation.

These artworks were created as part of a residency prorgamme at Hands-On, and were exhibited in 2026 during "(Un)learning friction" at Siegfried Contemporary, London, curated by Senem Cagla Bilgin-Keys.
