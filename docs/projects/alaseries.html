Ala series

Ala is a series of unique sculpted door handles that integrate the natural world into the home. Birds and the symbols associated with them refer to freedom, peace, and protection. Each piece has been finely carved in wood by artisans who make traditional altarpieces for churches. This series was designed in collaboration with Peca Studio, a design studio based in Guadalajara, Mexico.
